# Changelog

## 1.0.0

First release, for Fabric on Minecraft 26.3. Client side only.

- Wither spawn, ender dragon death and end portal open sounds play only when the event is within about 30 blocks of you, from the place where it happened.
- Events in another dimension are not heard.
- Each of the three sounds can be set to `everywhere` (vanilla), `nearby` (default) or `off` in `config/local-boss-sounds.json`.
